## Goal-Setting Principles

- Goals must be specific enough to evaluate honestly
- Each goal needs a clear "done" state, not just a direction
- Limit to 5-7 goals — more than that dilutes focus
- Every goal should connect to either the 5-year COO target or life quality
- Review monthly, adjust quarterly, evaluate annually
