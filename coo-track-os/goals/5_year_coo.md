## The Goal

Within five years, I will be ready for — and positioned to attain — the role of Chief Operating Officer at a pharmaceutical CDMO. Readiness means I can step into the role and perform, not just hold the title.

This document tracks the long arc. It connects daily actions to the five-year target. Every review cadence feeds into this.

---

## What COO Means

### The Role

A COO at a pharma CDMO typically:
- Reports to the CEO/President
- Owns end-to-end operations: manufacturing, quality, supply chain, and often engineering/facilities
- Co-owns commercial execution with the Commercial/Business Development leader
- Drives operational strategy aligned with business strategy
- Represents the company to clients, regulators, and the board
- Leads 200-2000+ employees across multiple functions
- Manages P&L responsibility or significant budget authority
- Champions operational excellence, continuous improvement, and digital transformation

### The Person

The person who succeeds as COO:
- Thinks in systems, not functions
- Earns trust across every department — not just their home function
- Makes decisions with incomplete information and lives with the consequences
- Balances operational rigor with strategic flexibility
- Develops leaders, not just manages teams
- Communicates at board level and shop floor level with equal effectiveness
- Navigates organizational politics without losing integrity

## The Five-Year Roadmap

### Year 1: Foundation and Cross-Functional Entry

**Theme:** Earn the right to be seen as more than an operations specialist.

**Key actions:**
- Establish meaningful relationships with Quality, Supply Chain, and Commercial leadership
- Complete BPR automation project — demonstrating operational innovation
- Build commercial acumen through deliberate learning: [DEVELOPMENT PRIORITIES]
- Formalize the Tooling Layer framework within the organization
- Grow content platform to establish external thought leadership

**COO-readiness target:** 2.5 / 5

---

### Year 2: Cross-Functional Expansion

**Theme:** Demonstrate judgment outside my home domain.

**Key actions:**
- Take on a cross-functional project or responsibility outside Operations
- Develop finance literacy to the point of contributing to budget and P&L discussions
- Build direct relationships with key customers/clients
- Mentor or develop a direct report into a leadership role
- Present at an industry conference or publish a significant thought leadership piece

**COO-readiness target:** 3.0 / 5

---

### Year 3: Strategic Leadership

**Theme:** Shift from functional leader to organizational leader.

**Key actions:**
- Lead or co-lead a significant organizational initiative (not just an operations initiative)
- Demonstrate board-level or executive-level communication capability
- Build a successor for the Operations Director role
- Expand scope — either through promotion, expanded responsibility, or organizational change
- Establish the Tooling Layer as recognized organizational infrastructure

**COO-readiness target:** 3.5 / 5

---

### Year 4: Executive Readiness

**Theme:** Close the remaining gaps. Position for the transition.

**Key actions:**
- Achieve Level 4+ in all cross-functional competencies
- Develop a clear operational vision for the organization (or a future organization)
- Build executive sponsorship for COO candidacy
- Assess: is the COO opportunity available here, or must I look externally?
- Financial and personal readiness for a major role transition

**COO-readiness target:** 4.0 / 5

---

### Year 5: Transition

**Theme:** Step in — or step up to the next opportunity.

**Key actions:**
- Pursue the COO role — internally or externally
- Demonstrate readiness through track record, relationships, and vision
- Negotiate the transition with clarity about what success looks like
- If the role is not available, make the strategic decision: wait, pivot, or create

**COO-readiness target:** 4.5+ / 5

## Decision Points Along the Way

### Year 2-3: The Organization Question

> "Is my current organization the right place to reach COO — or do I need to leave to grow?"

Factors to evaluate:
- Is a COO role likely to open here?
- Am I getting the cross-functional exposure I need?
- Do decision-makers see me as COO-potential?
- Is there a glass ceiling I'm not acknowledging?

### Year 3-4: The Scope Question

> "Am I growing fast enough — or am I comfortable?"

Comfort is the enemy of this goal. If Year 3 looks like a slightly better version of Year 1, something is wrong.

### Year 4-5: The Readiness Question

> "Am I actually ready, or am I just experienced?"

Experience and readiness are different. Experience accumulates passively. Readiness is built deliberately.
