## Strategic Insight

**One strategic observation from this week — about the business, the industry, or your leadership development:**

- In the AI-assisted era, the bottleneck is no longer execution — it's choosing the right approach upfront. AI offers multiple viable paths (Marker, PyMuPDF, Gemini OCR, custom Python), and each can produce results. But spending 2 weeks iterating on the wrong approach costs more than spending 1 day evaluating all options first. The strategic skill is: plan before iterating, evaluate approaches by time-to-goal, then commit. When co-working with AI, the cheapest resource is trying each approach briefly; the most expensive mistake is going deep on the wrong one.
