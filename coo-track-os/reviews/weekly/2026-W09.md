## Strategic Insight

**One strategic observation from this week — about the business, the industry, or your leadership development:**

- HBR article insight: The most critical factor for AI adoption in companies is not translation cost — it's **communication cost** between departments. To make data fluent and shareable across departments via AI, you must unify file formats. Different departments use PDFs, various systems, etc., but AI models read MD files best. The infrastructure play: convert all shareable knowledge and data into MD format so AI can facilitate cross-department communication.

  This connects to a second article from last week confirming MD files as the most machine-readable format. This is exactly why I'm pushing to convert SOPs, Work Instructions (WIs), and critical documents into MD files with internal linkages. The POC across 8 SOPs is the first step.

  **Reality check:** I am currently the only one with this specific awareness and AI experience. Pushing these initiatives alone will be difficult, and adoption will be slow at first. But it will accelerate once leadership demonstrates efficiency and people see the benefits. Wade's independent alignment on this exact philosophy — patience, demonstrate value first, let results speak — was a surprise and a major source of energy this week.

*Is this insight worth tracking in `memory.md`?* Yes — the "communication cost > translation cost" framework and the MD-as-infrastructure strategy are foundational to the AI roadmap.
