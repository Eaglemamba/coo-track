# Interview: Past Year Reflection

A guided self-interview for reflecting on the past year of operations leadership. Best conducted during the annual review or at year-end.

Read each question slowly. Write your response before moving to the next. Do not pre-read the questions — let each one land fresh.

---

## Setting the Stage

Find a quiet space. Block 60-90 minutes. No screens except this document. Write by hand if possible — the friction is intentional.

Before you begin, take three slow breaths. You are not being evaluated. You are being witnessed — by the most informed observer of your career: yourself.

---

## The Interview

### Opening

**Tell me about this past year. Not the highlight reel — the real version. What was it actually like to lead through this year?**

[Write freely]

---

### Operational Impact

**What moved the needle operationally this year? Not what was busy — what actually changed outcomes?**

[Write freely]

**What did you build this year that will still matter in three years?**

[Write freely]

**Where did operational urgency crowd out strategic thinking? Can you name specific moments?**

[Write freely]

---

### Leadership

**Describe a moment this year when your leadership was exactly what the situation needed.**

[Write freely]

**Now describe a moment when it wasn't. What happened? What did you miss?**

[Write freely]

**Where did you avoid a hard conversation with your team or peers? What was the cost of avoidance?**

[Write freely]

**Where did you default to doing instead of developing others? What drove that — urgency, ego, habit, or something else?**

[Write freely]

---

### Cross-Functional Growth

**What cross-functional relationship did you neglect this year?**

[Write freely]

**Where did you operate outside your comfort zone in Quality, Supply Chain, Commercial, Finance, or Regulatory? What did you learn?**

[Write freely]

**If the COO role opened tomorrow, which function's leadership team would be least confident in your ability to lead their area? Why?**

[Write freely]

---

### AI and Tooling Layer

**How did your AI tools and systems evolve this year? What worked, what didn't?**

[Write freely]

**Where did AI create the most operational leverage? Where did it create the most organizational friction?**

[Write freely]

**Did you maintain the 50% output rule — did time saved flow to creation, or did it get absorbed?**

[Write freely]

---

### Patterns and Habits

**Apply the three-question reflection to your entire year:**

*What patterns in your reasoning this year?*

[Write freely]

*Where did you get stuck — not externally, but in your own thinking?*

[Write freely]

*What thinking habits helped or hurt?*

[Write freely]

---

### The Uncomfortable Questions

**Where did you wait for the right timing — and was that wisdom or avoidance?**

[Write freely]

**What did you know needed to change in January that you still haven't changed?**

[Write freely]

**If this year repeated ten times, would you be ready for COO?**

[Write freely]

**What would your future COO self tell you to stop doing?**

[Write freely]

---

### Closing

**In one sentence, what was this year about?**

[Write freely]

**In one sentence, what must next year be about?**

[Write freely]

---

## After the Interview

1. Read through your responses
2. Highlight the 3 most surprising things you wrote
3. Extract key insights and add them to `memory.md`
4. Flag any responses that could develop into LinkedIn/Substack content
5. Use this interview as input for the full annual review in `frameworks/annual_review.md`

---

*This interview is designed to surface what you already know but haven't articulated. Articulation is diagnosis — if something is hard to write, that difficulty is the insight.*
