# Interview: Leadership Style

A guided self-interview for examining how you actually lead — not how you think you lead. The gap between self-perception and team experience is where the most valuable leadership development lives.

Best conducted semi-annually or after significant leadership moments (reorganizations, project completions, difficult team situations).

---

## Setting the Stage

This interview asks you to be specific. Not "I'm a collaborative leader" but "Last Tuesday, when the deviation came in, I took over the investigation instead of letting the team lead." Specificity prevents self-deception.

Block 45-60 minutes.

---

### The COO Lens

**COO leadership is fundamentally different from functional leadership. A COO:**
- Leads leaders, not individual contributors
- Optimizes the whole organization, not one function
- Navigates competing priorities without clear right answers
- Represents the company externally
- Makes decisions with incomplete information across unfamiliar domains

**Which of these is most natural for you? Which is most foreign?**

[Write freely]

**What must change about your leadership style to succeed at the COO level?**

[Write freely]
