# Interview: Identity and Values

A guided self-interview for clarifying who you are becoming — not just what you are accomplishing. Best conducted during the annual review or during career inflection points.

This interview explores the person behind the operator. The COO track is a professional goal, but the person who reaches it will be shaped by what they value, not just what they achieve.

---

## Setting the Stage

This is the most personal interview in the system. There are no right answers. There are only honest ones.

Block 60 minutes. Write slowly.
