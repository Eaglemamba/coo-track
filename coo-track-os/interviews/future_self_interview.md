# Interview: Future Self

A guided self-interview conducted from the perspective of your future self — five years from now, having achieved (or not achieved) the COO role. This interview uses the future vantage point to surface what your present self cannot see.

Best conducted quarterly, rotating with other interview scripts.

---

## Setting the Stage

Imagine you are five years in the future. You are sitting in a quiet office. You have the perspective of someone who has lived through the next five years of your career — the successes, the failures, the surprises.

Now, that future self is interviewing your present self.

Write your responses as your present self. Let the questions challenge you.

---

## The Interview

### From the Future

**I'm looking back at where you are now — [CURRENT DATE]. You're an Operations Director at a CDMO, leading BPR automation, building AI tools, creating content. Here's what I want to ask you:**

**What are you spending time on right now that won't matter in five years?**

[Write freely]

**What are you neglecting right now that you will wish you had started sooner?**

[Write freely]

**You have strong opinions about AI adoption, the Tooling Layer, the Builder Triad. Which of those opinions will turn out to be wrong?**

[Write freely]

### Career Path

**You want to be COO in five years. But you haven't fully considered what you're willing to give up for it. Let me ask: what did the COO path actually cost you?**

[Write freely — answer from your present knowledge, acknowledging what you don't yet know]

**If the COO role never materializes — if the path leads somewhere else — would this journey still be worthwhile? Why or why not?**

[Write freely]

**What role would make you happiest that you haven't allowed yourself to consider because it feels less ambitious than COO?**

[Write freely]

### The Hard Questions

**Here's the truth about your content practice — the LinkedIn posts, the Substack articles, the "AI Explorer" brand. Is it advancing your career or feeding your ego? How do you know the difference?**

[Write freely]

**You value timing over argument. But sometimes, waiting for the right moment is just a sophisticated form of avoidance. Where are you waiting right now when you should be acting?**

[Write freely]

**You believe in showing the process, not just conclusions. But leaders also need to project confidence. How will you resolve this tension at the COO level?**

[Write freely]

**If this year repeated five times, where do you actually land? Not where you hope — where does the math actually put you?**

[Write freely]

## After the Interview

1. Read the letter you wrote from your future self
2. Identify the single most important message
3. Determine one concrete action for this quarter based on that message
4. Extract insights to `memory.md`
5. Update `goals/5_year_coo.md` if your understanding of the path has shifted

---

*This interview works because temporal distance creates honesty. It is easier to tell the truth about the present when you are speaking from the future. The future self is not wiser — but they are more honest.*
