# COO-Readiness Assessment

An honest, structured evaluation of readiness for the Chief Operating Officer role at a pharmaceutical CDMO. This is not aspirational — it is diagnostic. The purpose is to identify exactly where you stand and what must change.

---

## Instructions

1. Complete this assessment quarterly
2. Rate yourself honestly — aspiration is for goals documents, not assessments
3. Gather input from trusted advisors when possible
4. Compare scores across quarters to track trajectory
5. Focus development energy on the highest-leverage gaps
