# Life Map

Adapted from Alex Lieberman's Life Map framework — six domains that constitute a complete life. The insight: professional success in isolation is not success. This map ensures the COO track does not come at the expense of the life it is supposed to serve.

---

## Instructions

1. Rate each domain 1-10 on current satisfaction
2. Write a brief honest assessment
3. Identify one action per domain for the next quarter
4. Update quarterly during the quarterly review
5. Look for domains that are consistently neglected — that is your blind spot
