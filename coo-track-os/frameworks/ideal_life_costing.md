# Ideal Lifestyle Costing

Adapted from Tim Ferriss' approach to lifestyle design — customized for a senior pharma operations leader. The insight: most people set income goals without defining what the income is for. This framework reverses the process — define the life first, then calculate what it costs.

---

## Instructions

1. Complete Part 1 first — before looking at numbers
2. Be honest about what you actually want, not what sounds reasonable
3. Revisit annually during the annual review
4. Use this to inform career decisions — not just financial ones
