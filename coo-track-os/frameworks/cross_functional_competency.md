# Cross-Functional Competency Map

A COO in pharma CDMO must have genuine fluency — not just awareness — across six functional domains. This tracker maps your current level, target level, and development path for each.

---

## Competency Levels

| Level | Definition | Evidence |
|-------|-----------|----------|
| 1 — Awareness | I know this function exists and what it broadly does | Can describe the function's purpose |
| 2 — Literacy | I understand the key concepts, vocabulary, and priorities | Can hold an informed conversation |
| 3 — Conversational | I can engage in substantive discussions and ask good questions | Can challenge ideas and identify gaps |
| 4 — Functional | I can make judgment calls in this domain with appropriate guidance | Can evaluate proposals and risks |
| 5 — Fluency | I can make independent judgment calls and lead cross-functional decisions | Can drive strategy and resolve conflicts |

COO-readiness requires Level 4-5 in all six domains.
