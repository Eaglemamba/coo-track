## Part 3: The Three-Question Deep Dive (30 min)

Apply the three-question reflection to the entire year:

### What patterns in my reasoning this year?

*Look across decisions, strategies, and reactions. What recurring logic shaped my choices?*

### Where did I get stuck this year?

*Not just obstacles — where did my own thinking create friction? What assumptions held me back?*

### What thinking habits helped or hurt this year?

*Which mental models served me? Which ones do I need to update or retire?*
