# Vivid Vision

Inspired by Tony Robbins' approach to vision clarity — adapted for an operations leader on the COO track. The premise: you cannot move toward something you cannot see clearly. Vague goals produce vague outcomes.

This is not a vision statement for a company. This is a vivid, detailed picture of your life and career three years from now. Write it in present tense, as though you are already living it.

---

## Instructions

1. Set a timer for 30 minutes
2. Write freely in present tense — "I am...", "My day looks like...", "I lead..."
3. Be specific. Not "I have a great team" but "I lead a team of 8 direct reports across 3 functions"
4. Include all domains, not just work
5. Read it aloud. Does it feel true and compelling? If not, rewrite.
6. Update annually during the annual review

## The Gap

After writing the vision, answer:

1. What is the single biggest gap between where I am now and this vision?
2. What is already true in this vision today?
3. What will be hardest to achieve?
4. What must I give up to get there?
