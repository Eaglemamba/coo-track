# Content Pipeline

Capturing publishable insights as they surface — from daily check-ins, weekly reviews, conversations, and projects. The pipeline moves ideas from raw capture to published content.

---

## How This Works

1. **Capture**: During any review or work session, when an insight feels worth sharing, record it here
2. **Develop**: Weekly, review captures and identify which ones have legs
3. **Draft**: Move promising ideas to draft stage — write a LinkedIn hook or Substack outline
4. **Publish**: Finalize and publish
5. **Reflect**: After publishing, note engagement and what resonated

**Content identity**: "AI Explorer in Pharma CDMO" — bilingual content (English and Traditional Chinese) exploring AI adoption in regulated pharmaceutical environments.

**Core content themes:** [CONTENT FOCUS]
- Builder Triad framework (Validation Designers, Pattern Analysts, Workflow Architects)
- Tooling Layer principle (AI as the fourth organizational capability)
- AI in GMP-regulated environments
- Operational leadership and the COO journey
- Walking + voice capture + Claude dialogue workflow
- "The value isn't in AI generating answers. It's in AI providing structured feedback on your answers."
