# Stakeholder Map

A strategic view of the relationships that matter for your COO transition and current operational effectiveness. This is not a networking exercise — it is a map of influence, trust, and development opportunity.

---

## How to Use This Map

1. Populate with your key stakeholders during initial setup
2. Review weekly — identify one relationship to invest in
3. Update monthly — has trust increased, decreased, or stalled?
4. During quarterly reviews — assess the overall health of your stakeholder network
