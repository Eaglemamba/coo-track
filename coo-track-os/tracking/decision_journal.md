# Decision Journal

A log of significant decisions — capturing context, reasoning, and outcomes over time. The purpose is not to judge past decisions but to improve future ones by studying patterns in how you decide.

---

## How to Use This Journal

1. Record a decision within 24 hours of making it
2. Fill in context, options considered, reasoning, and confidence level
3. Return after 30, 90, and 180 days to record outcomes
4. During quarterly reviews, analyze patterns across decisions

**What qualifies as a "significant decision":**
- Decisions that affect multiple people or functions
- Decisions that are difficult to reverse
- Decisions where you felt uncertain
- Decisions that relate to the COO track
- Decisions about technology adoption, process change, or organizational design
