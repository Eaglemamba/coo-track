# Delegation Log

Tracking what you release, how it goes, and what it reveals about your leadership development. Delegation is not task management — it is team development. Every entry here represents a development opportunity for someone on your team.

---

## How to Use This Log

1. When you delegate something significant, log it here
2. Record the intent — is this about efficiency, development, or both?
3. Follow up at 30 days — how did it go?
4. Review monthly — are you delegating enough? The right things? To the right people?
